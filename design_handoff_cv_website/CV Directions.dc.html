1Three directions for the CV scroll experience

All three share the opening screen's palette — deep charcoal ground, gold (#d4b262) accent, Helvetica body with a Newsreader serif for display. What differs is the layout system, how sections are separated, and how the live progress sidebar behaves. Each desktop mock is shown with the four scroll sections stacked (Profile → Experience → Education → Skills); the phone beside it shows the responsive sidebar treatment. Static previews — the real build adds the fade-and-slide reveals and scroll tracking.

1aTimeline Spine. Classic single centered column. Sidebar is a literal vertical spine — dots + labels joined by a progress line that fills as you scroll. Calm, recruiter-friendly, very legible.
